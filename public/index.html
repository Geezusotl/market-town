    Read the model’s assumptions

    60 households and six firms. Fixed workers also own their businesses. Two suppliers produce inputs; four firms turn inputs into goods. Wages, purchases and weekly owner distributions circulate a fixed supply of money. Prices adjust within declared bounds. No banks, taxes, credit, new businesses or real economic data. A firm closes after seven consecutive missed payrolls; fixed ownership and no new entry can make a shock’s effects permanent.

    Explanations show recorded dependencies and conditions, not proven real world causation. Seller attribution in the pooled market is bookkeeping, not physical lot tracing. The unchanged and disrupted towns are deterministic. They run in your browser, not on an unattended server.
